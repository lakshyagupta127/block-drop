3. Browser Fingerprinting

Block Drop uses a privacy-preserving browser fingerprint to identify you across sessions without requiring a login or email address. This fingerprint is a SHA-256 cryptographic hash generated from:

  • Your browser's user agent string
  • Your browser language setting
  • Your screen resolution and colour depth
  • Your timezone offset
  • Your CPU core count (hardware concurrency)
  • Your platform string (e.g. "Win32")

The raw values are never sent to our server — only the hashed result. This hash cannot be reversed to identify you personally. It is used solely to associate your display name with your game sessions.

You can reset your identity at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or using the "Delete My Data" option in Settings.

Block Drop requires users to confirm they are aged 13 or older before playing. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data to us, please contact us at [YOUR EMAIL] and we will delete it promptly.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • To display your name and score on the leaderboard
  • To associate your display name with your browser session (fingerprint)
  • To prevent cheating via score validation
  • To prevent abuse via rate limiting
  • To improve the game based on aggregate, anonymised usage patterns

We do not:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Share your data with any third party except as required by law
  • Store payment information (there are no in-app purchases)
  • Collect your real name, email, phone number, or physical address

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a SQLite database on our server. We protect it using:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • HMAC cryptographic signing to prevent score tampering
  • JWT-secured admin access
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Helmet.js security headers

8. Cookies & Local Storage

Block Drop does not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage to store:

  • Your browser fingerprint hash (player identity)
  • Your age confirmation status
  • Your sound/music volume preferences

This data never leaves your device unless explicitly submitted (e.g. when you set a display name or submit a score).
