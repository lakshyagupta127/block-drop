📦 Data Collected & Shared

Block Drop collects minimal data required to operate the leaderboard and prevent cheating.

✔ Name (display name — chosen by you)

✔ App activity (scores, level, lines)

✔ Device or other identifiers (browser fingerprint hash)

🖥 App settings (volume) — local only, not shared

👤 Data NOT Collected

✗ Location ✗ Email address ✗ Phone number ✗ Contacts ✗ Photos / media ✗ Health data ✗ Financial info ✗ Messages ✗ Microphone / camera

Data Deletion

You can delete all your stored data (display name, scores, fingerprint) at any time:

  • In-app: Settings (⚙️) → Delete My Data
  • By email: Contact us at [YOUR EMAIL] with subject "Data Deletion Request"

We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Third-Party Services

Block Drop does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or social login providers. All data stays on our own server.
